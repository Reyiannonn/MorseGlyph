# Privacy Policy for Morse Glyph

_Last updated: May 1, 2026_

Morse Glyph ("the app") is an open-source Android application that translates text into Morse code and transmits it through the Nothing Phone Glyph Matrix and audio. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information.

## Summary

**Morse Glyph does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data.** The app works entirely offline and has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

## Information the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device using Android's standard `SharedPreferences` storage. This data never leaves your device.

The locally stored information includes:

- Your preferred transmission speed (words per minute)
- Your preferred indicator mode (Symbol, Full, or Per-letter)
- Your last 10 transmitted messages, shown as quick-select chips for convenience
- A flag indicating whether you have completed the first-launch onboarding

This information is accessible only to the app itself and to you. It is not synced, backed up to any service operated by the developer, or transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions

The app requests the minimum permissions required to function:

- **Glyph Interface permission** — to display Morse patterns on the Nothing Phone Glyph Matrix
- **Wake lock** — to keep the CPU active during transmission so that turning the screen off does not interrupt playback

The app does not request access to the internet, contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data.

## Data sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties because it does not collect any data in the first place.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

All locally stored information is removed when you uninstall the app. There is no remote data to delete because none is ever transmitted off your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL where you are reading this. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change.
